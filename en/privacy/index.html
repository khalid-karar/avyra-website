Privacy policy

Effective: November 2026

1. Who we are

Avyra Company (Riyadh, Saudi Arabia) operates this website and its related services. References to "Avyra Company" and "we" mean this entity.

2. Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts and files are served from our own server, not third parties. Like any website, our hosting provider logs routine technical data (such as network address, date and time, and the page requested) for security and operations. If you write to us by email or the contact form, we receive your name, organization, contact details and the content of your message.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we receive to answer your enquiry, assess collaboration opportunities and keep a record of business correspondence. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

4. Where data is processed

Data sent through this website is handled in line with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and applicable SDAIA guidance.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data under applicable law. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@avyra.sa.

6. Contact

For any privacy question, write to privacy@avyra.sa.
